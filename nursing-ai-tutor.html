Agentic tutoring system

An eight-agent team, working for one learner.

Nightingale pairs a Learner Profiling Agent, a Clinical Reasoning tutor, a Quiz Generator, an Evidence-Based Feedback agent, a Learning Planner and a Progress Analyst — all coordinated by an Orchestration layer and grounded by a Knowledge & Reasoning layer before anything reaches you.

1 · Learner Profiling Agent

Captures your experience level (1–5), goals, strengths and weak spots so every other agent can personalize its output.

2 · Clinical Reasoning Chat

Describe a case or symptom. The tutor walks you through reasoning step by step, in plain language.

3 · Quiz Generator + 4 · Feedback

Pick a topic, difficulty and question count. Wrong answers get plain-language, evidence-based correction with a trustworthiness score.

5 · Learning Planner

Builds a paced weekly plan, recommends what to study next, and simulates where it leads.

6 · Progress Analysis Agent

Tracks scores, competency growth and trends across every quiz you take.

7 · Orchestrator & 8 · Knowledge Layer

Run silently behind every action: planning, executing, monitoring, reflecting — and grounding answers in curated nursing sources.
